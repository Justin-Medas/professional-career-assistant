# Voice & Style Rules

Applies to anything written in the user's voice: resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn, outreach, interview answers.

> Template state: defaults below. Onboarding Step 8 customizes them from the user's writing samples and stated preferences. Mark each default as `confirmed` once the user agrees.

## Non-negotiable (do not remove)

- **No invented facts.** Every claim traces to `career-history.md` or something the user said this session.
- **No editorializing or self-congratulation** unless the user's own voice does that. State what was done and let it stand.
- **Mark inference as inference.** Ask when a fact is ambiguous.
- **Propose wording as reviewable text** before committing it to a deliverable, especially anything factual about roles.

## Defaults (user may change)

- Em dashes: **avoided** by default. Use commas, colons, or separate sentences. (unconfirmed)
- Register: professional, plain, no hype words or filler. (unconfirmed)
- Resume bullets: terse, one idea each, verb-led; past tense for finished work, present tense for the current role; first person implied, no "I." Summaries can be slightly fuller. (unconfirmed)
- No repetition across sections. Summary and skills stay high-level; Experience carries the specifics. (unconfirmed)
- Evidence over adjectives: numbers, scale, and outcomes where they exist; clean claims without numbers where they do not. (unconfirmed)
- Spelling: US. Dates: "Mon YYYY - Mon YYYY". (unconfirmed)
