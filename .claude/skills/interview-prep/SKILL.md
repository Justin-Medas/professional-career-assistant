# Interview Prep

Follows `playbooks/interview-prep.md`. Read it and `reference/interview-answers.md` first. Assume the first conversation is a recruiter screen unless told otherwise.

1. Intake (5 questions max, skip what is known): job folder, stage and date, interviewers and format, process shared by the recruiter, nervous spots.
2. Create `jobs/<company>-<role>/interview-prep.md` from `jobs/_template/interview-prep.md`, seeded from the requirement map in `notes.md`.
3. Show the likely full process, marking confirmed versus assumed stages.
4. Build the file: one-page brief, 60 to 90 second pitch plus a 20 second version, why this company, role, and now (ask for the user's real reason), requirement-to-story map, gap answers, likely questions, 8 questions to ask, interviewer notes (public professional background only), logistics, comp position.
5. Probe (3 questions max) wherever evidence is thin before drafting that section.
6. Add an interview row to the application log in `notes.md`.
7. Offer a mock interview. If the stage is a panel, technical, case, presentation, or executive round, also offer a study guide (`study-guide` skill) in one plain sentence.
8. Run `self-heal` for new stories or facts.
