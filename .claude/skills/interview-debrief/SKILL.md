# Interview Debrief and Follow-up

Follows the Debrief and Follow-up sections of `playbooks/interview-prep.md` and `profile/voice-and-style.md`. Best within an hour of the interview; follow-ups are due within 24 hours.

1. Ask, a couple of questions at a time: interviewer names and titles, questions asked and how the user answered, what landed or stumbled, what they learned, concerns raised, next steps and timeline.
2. Log it in the debrief section of the job's `interview-prep.md` and update the application log in `notes.md`.
3. Route new facts into the profile (`self-heal`). Questions the panel actually asked go into the study guide's practice list if one exists.
4. Draft one follow-up per person, under 150 words, different content per panelist: thanks, one specific moment, fit in one line tied to their problem, a brief fix for any missed answer, a low-pressure next step.
5. Save drafts in markdown in the job folder. If a Gmail connector is available, offer to save as a Gmail draft.
6. Never send without the user's explicit yes for that specific message.
7. If the promised timeline passes, draft the silence follow-ups: after 2 business days, after about a week, then a close-the-loop note.
